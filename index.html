About

A decade-shaped path toward the details.

I started as a self-taught developer building tools for a university robotics lab, and found that the parts I loved most weren't the algorithms — they were the interfaces people used to trust them.

That pulled me toward design. Today I move fluidly between product strategy, interface design, and production-grade engineering — usually on the same project, often in the same week.

I've led front-end architecture for fintech dashboards, designed onboarding flows used by millions, and shipped design systems that outlived the teams that built them. I care about the 2% of a product that makes someone say "this feels different."
