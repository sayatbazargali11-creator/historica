About the academy

Built by people who love the subject.

SAYAT MATH started as after-school tutoring in Almaty and grew into a full online academy — the teaching philosophy hasn't changed.

Our mission

Mathematics as a way of thinking, not a subject to survive.

Most students meet mathematics as a set of procedures to memorise for a test. We teach it as a language for reasoning clearly — a skill that outlasts any single exam. Every lesson is built around a real question first, and a formula second.

What we hold to

Three principles behind every lesson.

Understanding before speed

We never trade comprehension for a faster answer. Speed is what happens naturally once a concept is genuinely understood.

Small live classes

Capped at 8 students so every learner is asked to reason out loud, not just watch a lecture happen.

Local roots, global standard

Aligned with Kazakhstan's national programme, benchmarked against international olympiad problem sets.

How we got here

A short history.

2019

After-school tutoring in Almaty

Started with two teachers and eleven students in a rented classroom, focused purely on olympiad preparation.

2021

First online cohort

Moved teaching online out of necessity, then discovered live small-group classes worked even better remotely.

2023

Full curriculum launch

Released the complete track system from arithmetic foundations through calculus, aligned to the national programme.

2026

5,000 students, 20 teachers

Grew into a full academy while keeping every live class capped at eight students.

SAYAT MATH

An online mathematics academy built in Kazakhstan, teaching mathematical thinking — not just formulas — to students of every level.
